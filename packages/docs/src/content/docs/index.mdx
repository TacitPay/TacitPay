## The idea in one table

A transparent chain publishes what you charged and who paid you. A fully anonymous one proves nothing to anyone. TacitPay holds both ends at once:

| Data                    | Public ledger                        | Payer             | Merchant       |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ----------------- | -------------- |
| Invoice amount          | **Hidden** (commitment only)         | Known (from link) | Known          |
| Memo / line items       | Hidden (hash inside commitment)      | Known             | Known          |
| Merchant identity       | Hidden (per-invoice tag, unlinkable) | Knows the sender  | —              |
| Payer identity          | Hidden (per-invoice tag)             | —                 | Not from chain |
| Invoice exists + status | **Public by design**                 | Public            | Public         |

Anyone can verify an invoice settled. Nobody can see what it was for.
